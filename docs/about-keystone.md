# About Keystone Security Distribution

**Your Mid-Atlantic Security Partner Since 1987**

---

## Who We Are

Keystone Security Distribution is a privately held wholesale distributor of locks, access control systems, automotive key products, safes, and locksmith supplies. Based in King of Prussia, Pennsylvania, we serve security professionals across Pennsylvania, New Jersey, Delaware, Maryland, Virginia, and Washington, D.C.

With annual revenue approaching $20 million, a team of 42 employees, and over 8,500 SKUs in stock, Keystone delivers the inventory depth and technical expertise that security professionals rely on.

---

## Our History

### 1987: Founded by a Locksmith, for Locksmiths

Robert "Bob" Hartwell spent two decades as a working locksmith before founding Keystone Security Distribution. Frustrated by long lead times and impersonal service from national distributors, Bob opened a 3,000-square-foot warehouse in King of Prussia with a simple promise: every order ships the same day, and every call gets answered by someone who knows the trade.

### 1990s: Building a Reputation

Through the 1990s, Keystone earned the trust of locksmith shops across southeastern Pennsylvania and southern New Jersey, one account at a time. Relationships built on reliability and product knowledge became the company's foundation.

### 2001: Expanding into Commercial and Access Control

The addition of commercial hardware and access control product lines opened Keystone's doors to security integrators and property management firms, broadening the customer base beyond traditional locksmith shops.

### 2008: New Headquarters

Keystone moved into a 45,000-square-foot headquarters facility on Commerce Drive in King of Prussia, tripling warehouse capacity and adding a dedicated technical support team.

### 2015: Cherry Hill Branch Opens

A second warehouse location in Cherry Hill, New Jersey, extended Keystone's same-day delivery reach into central New Jersey, Delaware, and the Baltimore-Washington corridor. The same year, Keystone launched its online ordering portal for 24/7 order placement and tracking.

### Today

Keystone serves over 150 active accounts across six states and the District of Columbia. The company remains privately held and guided by the same principles Bob Hartwell established nearly four decades ago.
