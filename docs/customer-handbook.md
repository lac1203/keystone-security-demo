# Keystone Security Distribution Customer Handbook

Welcome to Keystone Security Distribution. This handbook covers everything you need to know about working with us: opening an account, placing orders, shipping and delivery, returns, and getting technical support.

## Getting Started

### Opening an Account

To open a wholesale account with Keystone, you will need:

1. A valid business license or contractor license
2. Two trade references
3. A completed W-9 form
4. A completed credit application

**How to apply:**
- Call our sales team at **(610) 555-0187**
- Email your application to **newaccounts@keystonesecurity.com**
- Complete the application through our website

Most applications are reviewed and approved within two to three business days. Upon approval, you will receive your account number, login credentials for the online ordering portal, and an introduction to your assigned territory representative.

### Account Types

| Customer Type | Description | Typical Credit Limit |
|---------------|-------------|---------------------|
| Locksmith Shop (LSH) | Full-service locksmith businesses | $5,000 - $50,000 |
| Security Integrator (INT) | Access control and security system installers | $25,000 - $200,000 |
| Property Manager (PMG) | Residential and commercial property management firms | $5,000 - $30,000 |
| Hardware Retailer (RET) | Hardware stores and home improvement retailers | $10,000 - $75,000 |

### Payment Terms

| Terms | Description | Eligibility |
|-------|-------------|-------------|
| COD | Cash on delivery or prepayment | All new accounts |
| Net 15 | Payment due 15 days from invoice | After credit approval |
| Net 30 | Payment due 30 days from invoice | Established accounts with good history |
| Net 45 | Payment due 45 days from invoice | High-volume accounts by arrangement |

We accept Visa, MasterCard, and American Express for all orders. Credit card orders are processed at the time of shipment.

## Placing Orders

### Order Methods

- **Online Portal:** Place and track orders 24/7 at your convenience. Real-time inventory levels displayed for every product.
- **Phone:** Call **(610) 555-0187**, Monday through Friday, 7:00 AM to 5:00 PM Eastern.
- **Email:** Send orders to **orders@keystonesecurity.com** with your account number and PO number.
- **Territory Representative:** Your rep can take orders during scheduled visits or by phone.

### Same-Day Shipping Cutoff

Orders placed by **2:00 PM Eastern**, Monday through Friday, ship the same business day. Orders received after 2:00 PM or on weekends are processed the next business day.

During peak season (May through August), we recommend placing orders before noon to ensure same-day fulfillment.

### Order Minimums

- **Established accounts:** No minimum order requirement
- **New accounts:** $50 minimum on the first three orders
- **Manufacturer minimums:** Some items (key blanks, credential cards) have manufacturer-set minimum quantities noted in the product listing

### Purchase Orders

Approved credit accounts can place orders using their own PO numbers. Your PO number appears on our invoice and packing slip for easy reconciliation. PO terms follow your account's established payment terms.

### Volume and Project Pricing

Orders exceeding standard quantity thresholds qualify for project pricing. Contact your territory representative or our sales desk with the SKU list and quantities for a custom quote, typically returned within one business day.

### Standing Orders

For consumable items you reorder regularly (key blanks, cylinders, credential cards), we can set up a recurring order schedule with fixed quantities and preferred ship dates. Ask your territory representative to configure a standing order for your account.

### Local Delivery

We operate our own delivery trucks from both the King of Prussia and Cherry Hill locations. Local deliveries placed by 2:00 PM typically arrive the same day or by the following morning. There is no delivery charge on local orders over $100.

**Local delivery zones:**
- King of Prussia: Philadelphia metro, Allentown, Reading, Lancaster, Wilmington
- Cherry Hill: South Jersey, central NJ, northern Delaware, Baltimore metro

### Will-Call Pickup

Both locations have will-call counters open Monday through Friday, 7:00 AM to 5:00 PM. Place your order online or by phone, select the will-call option, and you will receive a notification when your order is ready, usually within one hour.

### Tracking

Tracking numbers are emailed automatically when your order ships. View tracking status anytime in your online portal under Order History. For local deliveries, call our dispatch team for real-time estimated arrival windows.

### Safe and Equipment Shipping

Safes, key machines, and other heavy items ship via LTL freight carrier. Lift-gate delivery is available for locations without a loading dock. Contact us for freight quotes before ordering heavy equipment.

## Returns & Exchanges

### Return Policy

| Timeframe | Condition | Restocking Fee |
|-----------|-----------|---------------|
| Within 30 days | Original, unopened packaging | None |
| 31-60 days | Original, unopened packaging | 15% |
| After 60 days | Not accepted | N/A |

### Non-Returnable Items

The following items cannot be returned:
- Special-order products
- Keyed cylinders and cut keys
- Opened credential packs
- Used or installed hardware
- Clearance and discontinued items

### How to Initiate a Return

1. Contact customer service by phone or through the online portal
2. Request a Return Merchandise Authorization (RMA) number
3. Include the RMA number on the shipping label
4. Ship the item to the address provided with the RMA

**All returns must include an RMA number.** Unauthorized returns may be refused at the warehouse.

### Damaged or Incorrect Shipments

Report damaged or incorrect items within 48 hours of delivery. We will:
- Arrange a prepaid return label at no cost to you
- Ship the correct or replacement item immediately
- Process the return credit upon receipt of the original shipment

If the item is needed urgently, we can ship the replacement first and process the return separately.

### Warranty Claims

Most products carry the manufacturer's warranty. To file a claim:
1. Contact our technical support team at **(610) 555-0192**
2. Provide the product SKU, purchase date, and description of the issue
3. We will coordinate the claim with the manufacturer on your behalf
4. You will receive a replacement or credit as applicable
