## Residential Locks

Our residential lock line covers the full range of home security hardware, from entry-level builder packs to premium smart locks.

### Deadbolts (RES-DBL)
Single cylinder, double cylinder, and jimmy-proof deadbolts in ANSI/BHMA Grade 1 through Grade 3. Available in Satin Nickel, Aged Bronze, Matte Black, Polished Brass, and Polished Chrome finishes. Featured lines include the Schlage B-Series and Kwikset 660/980.

**Selection tips:**
- Grade 1 for exterior doors requiring maximum security
- Grade 2 for standard residential exterior doors
- Double cylinder where glass sidelights are present (check local code for egress requirements)
- Jimmy-proof deadlocks for apartment doors with surface-mount installation

### Entry Knobs (RES-KNB)
Entry, privacy, and passage function knobs. Popular designs include Schlage Plymouth, Kwikset Tylo, and Yale Georgian. Privacy knobs for bathrooms and bedrooms; passage knobs for closets and hallways.

### Handlesets (RES-HND)
Decorative front entry handlesets that combine a grip handle with a deadbolt. Schlage Camelot, Kwikset Century, and Weiser Addison series stocked in multiple finishes for upscale residential projects.

### Smart Locks (RES-SMT)
Wi-Fi, Bluetooth, and Z-Wave enabled locks with keypad, touchscreen, and fingerprint entry. Compatible with Apple HomeKit, Amazon Alexa, and Google Home platforms. Featured products include the Schlage Encode Plus and Kwikset Halo.

**Selection tips:**
- Wi-Fi locks for standalone smart home integration (no hub required)
- Z-Wave locks when integrating with a smart home hub
- Fingerprint models for customers who want keyless, codeless entry

### Keypad Locks (RES-KPD)
Battery-powered keypad deadbolts and levers with programmable user codes. No wiring required for retrofit installation. Schlage BE365 and FE595 series.

## Commercial Hardware

Professional-grade door hardware meeting ANSI/BHMA Grade 1 standards for commercial and institutional applications.

### Cylindrical Locks (COM-CYL)
Heavy-duty cylindrical locksets in all standard commercial functions: classroom (F05), storeroom (F07), office (F08), entrance (F09), and privacy (F76). Corbin Russwin CL3300 and Sargent 10-Line.

**Function reference:**
- **Classroom (F05):** Key locks/unlocks outside lever; inside lever always free
- **Storeroom (F07):** Key required to retract latch from outside; inside lever always free
- **Office (F08):** Turn button locks/unlocks outside lever; key overrides
- **Entrance (F09):** Turn button locks/unlocks outside lever; key overrides; inside lever always free

### Mortise Locks (COM-MOR)
Grade 1 mortise locksets for heavy-use commercial and institutional openings. Corbin Russwin L9000 and Sargent 8200 series. Higher security and durability than cylindrical locks, required by many commercial specifications.

### Exit Devices (COM-EXT)
Rim, surface vertical rod, and concealed vertical rod panic hardware. Von Duprin 98/99 and Falcon 25 series. Fire-rated models available for UL-labeled openings.

**Selection tips:**
- Rim devices for single doors with standard frames
- Surface vertical rod for double doors without a center mullion
- Fire exit hardware (not panic hardware) required on fire-rated openings
- Verify door width: standard devices fit 36" doors; narrow stile devices for aluminum storefront frames

### Door Closers (COM-CLS)
Surface-mounted closers with adjustable backcheck, sweep, and latch speed. LCN 4040XP and Norton 7500 series. ADA-compliant models with regulated opening force not exceeding 5 lbf.

### Hinges (COM-HNG)
Full mortise, spring, continuous, and pivot hinges. Standard weight for interior doors; heavy weight for exterior and high-frequency doors. McKinney, Hager, and Ives.

**Sizing guide:**
- 4.5" x 4.5" for standard commercial doors up to 3'0" x 7'0"
- Three hinges per door up to 7'0" height; add one hinge for each additional 2'6"
- Non-removable pins (NRP) for out-swinging exterior doors

### Thresholds (COM-THR)
Saddle, thermal break, and ADA ramp thresholds in aluminum and bronze. Standard 36" and 48" lengths. Pemko and Zero International profiles. ADA-compliant thresholds with maximum 1/2" height and beveled edges.

## Access Control

Components for electronic access control systems, from single-door standalone locks to multi-door networked platforms.

### Card Readers (ACC-RDR)
HID iCLASS SE and multiCLASS SE readers in wall-mount and mullion-mount form factors. Multi-technology readers support proximity (125 kHz), iCLASS (13.56 MHz), SEOS, and mobile credentials simultaneously.

### Controllers (ACC-CTL)
IP-based access controllers supporting 1 to 8 doors per panel. PoE-powered models reduce installation wiring. Scalable from a single entrance to campus-wide deployments.

### Credentials (ACC-CRD)
Proximity cards, key fobs, wristbands, and SEOS smart cards in bulk packs. Custom printing and encoding available. Compatible with HID iCLASS and multiCLASS reader platforms.

### Electric Strikes (ACC-STR)
12VDC, 24VDC, and 12/24VAC models for cylindrical and mortise lock applications. HES 1006 and Securitron UnlockIt series. Fail-safe (unlocks on power loss) and fail-secure (locks on power loss) models.

**Selection tips:**
- Fail-safe for fire-rated egress doors (door must unlock for emergency exit)
- Fail-secure for perimeter doors where security is the priority
- Verify strike compatibility with the lock function and door frame preparation

### Maglocks (ACC-MAG)
600 lb and 1200 lb holding force electromagnetic locks. Surface-mount and shear lock configurations. Bond sensor and LED status options for door position monitoring.

### Keypad Locks (ACC-KPD)
Commercial-grade standalone and networked keypad locks. Alarm Lock Trilogy T2 and T3 series with audit trail, scheduled access, and up to 3,000 user codes.

## Automotive

Products supporting automotive locksmith services: key duplication, transponder programming, and vehicle lockout.

### Transponder Keys (AUT-TRN)
Chip-embedded key blanks for Toyota, Honda, Ford, GM, BMW, Mercedes, Nissan, Chrysler, and other makes. Cloneable and OEM-programmable options. Ilco, JMA USA, and Strattec blanks.

### Programming Tools (AUT-PRG)
Key programming and diagnostic equipment. Autel IM608 Pro and IM508 Pro for comprehensive coverage. Smart Pro for high-volume shops. EEPROM tools for all-keys-lost scenarios.

### Lockout Tools (AUT-LOT)
Non-destructive vehicle entry tools: slim jims, long-reach tools, air wedges, and complete lockout kits. Designed for professional use with minimal risk of vehicle damage.

### Remotes & Fobs (AUT-RMT)
Remote head keys and smart proximity fobs for popular vehicle makes. Three- and four-button configurations. OEM-compatible replacements at wholesale pricing.

## Safes & Security

Residential and commercial safes with UL-listed fire and burglary ratings.

### Residential Safes (SAF-RES)
Home and office safes with digital keypad, biometric, or key lock access. Fire ratings from 30 minutes to 2 hours. Compact wall safes and mid-size floor models.

### Commercial Safes (SAF-COM)
UL-rated commercial safes with TL-15 and TL-30 burglary protection. Large-capacity models for cash, documents, and high-value inventory. AMSEC and Hollon.

### Gun Safes (SAF-GUN)
Fire-rated gun safes in 12 to 48 long gun capacities. Liberty Centurion, Colonial, and Fatboy Jr models with adjustable interior configurations.

### Deposit Safes (SAF-DEP)
Front-loading depository safes with drop slots and rotary hoppers for retail cash handling. B-rated construction. Dual key, digital, and combination lock options.

**Shipping note:** Safes ship via LTL freight. Lift-gate delivery available. Contact us for freight quotes on multi-unit orders.

## Key Machines & Supplies

Equipment and consumables for key cutting, rekeying, and lock servicing.

### Key Machines (KEY-MCH)
Manual, semi-automatic, and laser key cutting machines. Ilco Speed 044 (manual) and Speed 046 (semi-auto) for standard flat keys. Silca Futura Pro and Ninja Laser for high-security dimple and laser track keys.

### Key Blanks (KEY-BLK)
Residential and commercial blanks in all popular keyways. Schlage SC1/SC4, Kwikset KW1/KW10, Yale Y1, Best A, and restricted keyways including Medeco and Primus. Sold individually and in bulk boxes of 50 and 250.

### Pinning Kits (KEY-PIN)
Universal (.003 increment) and manufacturer-specific pinning kits. Bottom pins, master pins, top pins, and springs in labeled compartment trays. Schlage, Kwikset, Corbin Russwin, and universal kits.

### Lubricants (KEY-LUB)
Lock-specific lubricants: graphite powder, Teflon spray, penetrating oil, and multi-purpose lock spray. Formulated to avoid attracting dust and debris inside cylinders.

### Tools (KEY-TLS)
Professional locksmith hand tools: pick sets, plug followers, cylinder cap removers, pin tweezers, shim sets, and broken key extractors. Peterson, SouthOrd, HPC, and Pro-Lok.
